1. What this program covers

Yellowfone answers the phone for restaurants. When you call a restaurant that uses Yellowfone to place a takeout order, an automated assistant takes your order on the restaurant's line.

This page covers one thing only: the receipt for that order. It is the messaging program described in our carrier registration, and it is separate from any other messaging Yellowfone operates. Nothing on this page describes marketing, promotions, or a subscription, because this program sends none.

For how we handle your information generally, see our Privacy Policy. For the terms covering the service as a whole, see our Terms & Conditions.

2. How you opt in

Consent is given out loud, on a call you placed. There is no web form, no sign-up box, and no keyword to text. The only way to join this program is to call a restaurant and say yes.

After the assistant has read your full order back and you have confirmed it, it asks a separate question — reading your number aloud digit by digit so you can hear it and correct it before you answer:

"Should I send the receipt to five-one-two… five-five-five… one-two-three-four, the number you're calling from?"

  • Saying yes is your consent, for that one number and that one receipt.
  • Saying no means nothing is sent. Your order is still placed, and you can still pick it up as planned.
  • A different number is fine — give it, and it is read back to you the same way before anything is sent.
  • Consent does not carry over. Order again next week and you will be asked again.

3. What you receive

One text message, sent immediately after your order is placed. It looks like this:

Sample message

Thanks Alex! Your Restaurant Name order is confirmed. Order #305 · Pickup in ~25 min • 1× Chicken Dum Biryani (hot) • 1× Garlic Naan Total: $20.48 (incl. tax) Questions? restaurant's phone Reply STOP to opt out.

The highlighted parts are filled in with the restaurant you actually ordered from. The message is sent from a number registered to that restaurant.

4. Message frequency

The whole policy, in one line

One message per completed order.

Not one per item, not a reminder when it's ready, not a follow-up asking how it was. If you place three orders in a month and agree each time, you get three messages. If you never order again, you never hear from us again.

5. Message and data rates

Standard message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan. Yellowfone does not charge you for the receipt, and is not responsible for charges your carrier applies.

6. How to stop

Reply STOP to any receipt. You will get one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and then nothing further. Opting out applies to the number you reply from.

Opting out of receipts does not affect your ability to order. You can still call the restaurant and place an order exactly as before — you simply will not be texted.

7. Getting help

Reply HELP to any message, or email info@kypertech.com.

For anything about the food itself — a wrong item, a missing item, a change to the order, or when it will be ready — call the restaurant directly. Their number is on every receipt. Yellowfone answers the phone; the restaurant makes the food.

8. Carriers

Messaging is supported on major U.S. carriers, including AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon, Boost, Cricket, MetroPCS, U.S. Cellular, and others. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

9. What we do with your number

Your mobile number is used to deliver the receipt for the order you just placed. That is the only thing this program uses it for.

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, and consent data collected through this program are not sold, rented, or shared with any third party for marketing, promotion, or independent use. The only parties who see your number are the restaurant fulfilling your order and the messaging provider that delivers the text on our behalf.

Full detail on retention and your rights is in the Privacy Policy.
